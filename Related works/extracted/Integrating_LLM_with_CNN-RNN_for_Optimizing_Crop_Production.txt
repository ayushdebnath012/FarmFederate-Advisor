Abstract—This study establishes a hybrid methodology 
combining LLM, CNN, and RNN for predicting crop yield, and 
detailed coverage of each area of the hybrid is set as follows-. In 
general, traditional methods have great difficulties with 
analyzing agricultural data presented as complex non-linear 
data. Our suggested approach utilizes the extraction capabilities 
of CNN, the temporal sequence modeling of RNN, and the 
sophisticated data processing of LLM, and this hybrid approach 
produced high accuracy levels for tested crops including wheat, 
maize, millet, rice, and barley, as well as significantly better 
precision, recall and f-score cutoff levels than traditional 
methods.  This research contributes to ongoing developments 
toward improved food security and agriculture output through 
the implementation of sophisticated machine learning models. 
This paper presents a novel hybrid model for predicting crops 
yields by combining Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs) and 
Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) with Large Language 
Models (LLMs). Because agricultural data is challenging to 
model as traditional algorithms typically struggle with complex, 
inherently non-linear data, our modeling approach leverages 
the capabilities of sophisticated LLMs for processing data, 
temporal sequence modeling capabilities of RNNs, and data 
extraction capabilities of CNNs. Our hybrid model can produce 
high accuracy outcomes from the model experimentation using 
standard measures of output accuracy from a number of crop 
species, including barley, rice, millet, maize and wheat, and the 
model also produced significant and meaningful improvements 
over previous studies with traditional methods.  
 Keywords—Artificial Intelligence, large language model,

I. INTRODUCTION  
Successive prediction of the yields of crops is crucial in 
agricultural management and achievement of foodsecurity in 
particular regions. Yield forecasts are useful for farmers, 
policy makers and researchers, as they are used to allocate 
resources effectively, improve the techniques of agricultural 
production, and to know when there may be shortages of or 
surplus, food. Given the fact that the population of human 
beings in the global world is on increase, and hence the speed 
of food production, the agricultural trade waits for its future in

increase agronomic productivity. In many ways, the 
incorporation of LLMs in agriculture has benefits. This is so 
because they can handle big data, which includes previous 
crop yields, climatic conditions, soil types, and prior research 
studies on the subject. Implicit within these data are plant 
growth necessary qualities; the suitable crops to grow that 
could easily be seen from a particular location and the most 
effective means of dealing with rampant pests and diseases. 
Furthermore, LLMs are also helpful in supporting near real 
time decision making on the basis of current market situation, 
weather updates and the new innovation in the agricultural 
practices. This facility enables both farmers and agronomists 
to quickly decide and utilise input and other resources 
efficiently and minimizing risks which are inherent with the 
business. However, their capabilities Remains quite high, 
though usage in agriculture has not been as popular as in such

However, the potential here to bring about better efficiency in

promise of new systems like KissanGPT and their ability to 
outperform human professionals shows that large language 
models have a chance to transform agricultural systems. This 
analysis took place using new datasets collected specifically 
for the purpose of multimodal testing and operationalisation 
of an LLM based model in agriculture - we compiled data on 
agricultural and environmental variables across 3 formats and 
did large trials using GPT-4 to generate yield estimation from 
our datasets. GPT-4 was engaged in the process through 
specially constructured, precise prompt statements for 
numerical output. Following that, GPT-4 output was 
compared to disaggregated and collected annual real yield 
data, to assess whether the ability to rapidly estimate yield 
based on available dataset variables, could provide a reliable 
predictor framework. At the end of our assessments and

sustainably and productively in agriculture. LLMs and AI
