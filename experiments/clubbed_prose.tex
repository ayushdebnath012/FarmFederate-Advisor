\subsection{Unimodal and Multimodal Systems, Centralized and Federated}

Tables~\ref{tab:unimodal_cent_fed} and~\ref{tab:multimodal_cent_fed} place a
classical feature baseline, five frozen text encoders, five frozen vision
encoders, and their fusions on the same 222/74/75 crop--note support, each
scored centrally and again under FedAvg. Reading them in that order answers two
separate questions: what each modality is worth on its own, and what combining
them adds.

\textbf{Unimodal.} With complete notes the text branch is already at the
ceiling: frozen DistilBERT reaches 1.000 test accuracy and the image branch,
whose best encoder is ViT-Base at 0.733, trails it by 0.267. Federating costs
little on either side --- 1.000 to 0.987 for text and 0.733 to 0.667 for image,
against a $\pm0.10$ Wilson half-width on 75 crops --- so the deployment mode is
not what separates these systems; the modality is. The classical rows locate
where pretraining actually earns its place. On images it is decisive: colour
histograms with HOG reach 0.440 against ViT-Base's 0.733. On text it is not.
TF-IDF with a linear SVM is the strongest text row at every note sparsity
(1.000, 0.933, 0.733), beating all five transformer encoders, which is direct
evidence that the note channel is carried by lexical form rather than by
contextual meaning and is consistent with the leakage audit of
Section~\ref{sec:dataset}. A bag of words also degrades gracefully when words
are removed, whereas a mean-pooled contextual embedding does not.

\textbf{Multimodal.} At complete notes fusion cannot demonstrate anything:
ViT-Base with DistilBERT scores 1.000, the same as text alone, because there is
no headroom above a saturated branch. The comparison becomes informative only
once notes are incomplete, which is also the realistic field condition. At 75\%
deletion the ordering separates and fusion leads outright --- 0.800 for
ViT-Base with BERT-medium against 0.733 for the best image-only encoder and
0.653 for the best text-only encoder --- and the same holds encoder-for-encoder
at 50\% deletion, where ViT-Base with DistilBERT scores 0.800 against 0.707 for
DistilBERT alone and 0.733 for ViT-Base alone. Tables~\ref{tab:unimodal_cent_fed}
and~\ref{tab:multimodal_cent_fed} fuse by plain concatenation, which is the
weakest rung of Table~\ref{tab:mm_ladder}; with the deep-feature and calibrated
late-fusion rungs the same support gives 0.907 and 0.920 at 50\% deletion,
above the best text rung (0.827) and the best single visual model (0.680).
Federation preserves the ordering rather than creating it: at 75\% deletion the
federated fusion holds 0.751 while federated text falls to 0.582.

Figure~\ref{fig:clubbed_modality} summarizes both halves. Panel~(a) shows that
the classical-versus-pretrained gap is modality-dependent, and panel~(b) that
the three modalities are indistinguishable only while the notes are complete
--- the text and fusion curves coincide exactly at 1.000 --- and fan out as
notes are removed, with the image branch flat by construction and fusion the
only branch that is never worst.
