
The same ladder is run for text and for text${+}$image on the identical
222/74/75 crop--note support, so rows in Tables~\ref{tab:text_ladder}
and~\ref{tab:mm_ladder} are comparable with each other and with the visual
table. Two differences from the visual case are immediate. First, at complete
notes both ladders are flat: a single-candidate path already reaches 0.987
accuracy for text and 1.000 for fusion, and searching 3125 or 243 candidates
adds at most one crop, whereas the visual ladder gains 0.107 accuracy across
the same span. The binding constraint on the text side is therefore the corpus,
not the pipeline. Second, the ladders separate once notes are degraded, and the
ordering is stable: last-four-layer concatenation is the best text rung at both
50\% and 75\% deletion, and deep-feature concatenation the best fusion rung, so
representation depth is the choice that matters and is measurable only after
the shortcut is removed. The gates behave as the visual table already warns:
at 50\% deletion the three-expert gate has the joint-best validation accuracy
(0.932) and the worst test accuracy (0.747), a 0.185 gap against roughly 0.03
for the single models, which is the expected cost of fitting 243 routings on
74 validation crops.
